## What does NOT reproduce, and why

**1. The magnitude (+189 here vs +225 claimed) is not comparable.** The corpus was destroyed by
today's 08:41 reboot and rebuilt from sources; it is a declared NEW BASELINE (281 models,
1855 truth cylinders, against the historical 57 / 474). `SpeedTestStructure.step` here has 285
truth cylinder faces and 6,216 triangles at `normal`, against the lost file's 246 and 5,964 — it
is a different file. Comparing the two totals would be comparing two different measurements.

**2. "0 worse" FAILS on the negatives: it is 2.**

| model | truth cyl | A | B |
|---|---|---|---|
| `NEG_SOT-1334-1_fine` | 1 | 1 | **0** |
| `NEG_SOT-23W_Handsoldering_fine` | 12 | 1 | **0** |

Checked against truth before calling them regressions: on a designed-polygon negative, building
FEWER cylinders can be correct, so the count alone is not the test. Both of these have real
cylinders in their source CAD and both lose one. They are genuine losses.

Most likely they are outside DeepSeek's negative set: its gates ran on 54 files, this ran on the
whole 78-model `Package_TO_SOT_SMD` family. Its claim can be true on its set and still false
here. **That is exactly the hazard the never-written negatives manifest was meant to remove**, and
it is now concrete: a promotion gated on `models worse = 0` reads **2** on the only negative set
anyone can currently reproduce.

## Verdict

The recall work is **corroborated in direction, in mechanism and in its specific per-model
recoveries**, on an independently rebuilt corpus with an independently built binary. It is a large,
real gain (+179 on the main set with zero regressions and 94 % byte-identical output).

It is **not clean under the promotion gate as written**, because of the two negative rows.

Options, none taken — promotion is Tommaso's call:
1. Investigate the two rows and fix (they may share one cause; both are SOT-family).
2. Re-scope the negative set to a defined manifest and re-gate — but define it before measuring,
   never after seeing which members fail.
3. Waive them explicitly as a recorded, accepted regression of 2 cylinders against +189.

Do NOT pick (2) by dropping the two failing models. That is fitting the gate to the result.

## ADDENDUM 2026-09-11 — the two negative rows were not real cylinders

The verdict above rests on counting analytic cylindrical faces in the written STEP. That
metric cannot distinguish a recovered CAD cylinder from an invented one, and measurement since
shows the distinction matters here. Re-measured with `scripts/radius_audit.py`, which classifies
each built cylinder face by whether its radius exists in `truth.json` (within `max(0.5 %, 1e-4)`):

| set | n5h matched | n6 matched | delta | worse | unmatched n5h → n6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| main 203 | 187 | 212 | **+25** | **0** | 15 → 169 |
| negatives 78 | 253 | 265 | **+12** | **0** | 14 → 12 |
| total | 440 | 477 | **+37** | **0** | 29 → 181 |

- `NEG_SOT-1334-1_fine`: n5h's single cylinder is **R = 0.1556** against a truth radius of
  **0.15**. Unmatched. `NEG_SOT-23W_Handsoldering_fine`: **R = 0.5144** against `{0.1, 0.28}`.
  Unmatched. **Neither loss is a regression**, and the "0 worse FAILS on the negatives: it is 2"
  finding above is withdrawn.
- The `+189` is **+37 truth-matched**; the remaining +152 is geometry with no counterpart in
  the source CAD. `SpeedTest 0 → 130` is `0 → 3` matched; `L10_cross_slide 0 → 23` is `0 → 1`;
  `L04_soap_bar 0 → 12` is fully real.

Full derivation, and the `refineCylinderRadius` / G2 mechanism behind the fabrication, in
