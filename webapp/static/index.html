Options

Engine

Faceted = one CAD face per triangle (exact). TrueForm detects planes and cylinders and rebuilds analytic faces — slower on large meshes.

Repair mesh

Off = exact, geometry-preserving. Weld merges duplicate/split vertices, drops duplicate faces, and fixes winding. "Fill" also closes holes (best-effort via trimesh). Solidify forces a watertight solid by reconstruction (geometry changes; needs pymeshfix). Still-non-watertight meshes are reported honestly.

Merge co-planar

Off = exact faceted output (one face per triangle). On = smaller face count, no longer 1:1 with the mesh.

Welcome to mesh2step

Turn a 3D model into real CAD geometry you can open, measure and edit.

  1. 📤
    Drop your modelSTL, OBJ, 3MF or PLY — from a scanner, a download, anywhere.
  2. →
  3. ✨
    ConvertHoles become real circles, walls become real surfaces — not thousands of triangles.
  4. →
  5. ⬇️
    Open it in CADA STEP file that Fusion, SolidWorks, FreeCAD and Onshape treat as real geometry.

Need to trim the model, repair a messy scan, or pick a STEP version? They are under Options. Uploads are deleted after an hour.
